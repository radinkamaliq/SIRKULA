The food redistribution process

  1. Seller lists surplus food with condition & pickup deadline.
  2. Pickup deadlines help users prioritize listings that need attention.
  3. Receiver requests an available quantity.
  4. Seller approves or rejects; the demo updates available stock locally.
  5. Receiver picks up; seller marks completion.

Who benefits

  • Sellers: Reduce waste disposal costs; reach community buyers.
  • Receivers: Access safe, affordable surplus food.
  • Community: Less food waste, better food security.

SIRKULA does not replace official food safety inspections or regulations. Listings are not certified safe without verification.
